Confidence: **High (174)** = consumer, initializer, typed field, or linker equation establishes the role; **Medium (45)** = supported role with incomplete semantics/ownership; **Low (22)** = conservative label based on placement, weak use, or competing layouts. These are inferred descriptive names, not recovered original names.

Scope excludes standard input constants `D_JPAD` and `D_CBUTTONS`, documentation examples, ignored generated scratch files, and generated assembly/build outputs. Repeated spellings across regions are one row; different regional addresses with the same role receive the same proposed name for their respective builds. Segmented addresses identify locations in a loaded bank and are not universal object identities.

Evidence comes from local definitions, initializers, pointer tables, consumer routines, regional counterparts, and linker equations. Source references are representative declaration/use sites. ## Findings that affect renaming

- `D_800D7650` is defined as `RaceUiRankTextRenderEntry` but consumed as `RaceCoursePropModelEntry`. `D_800DBD10` is a packed word block in the audio file consumed as replay course-grid records. Resolve these competing views before choosing authoritative types.
- `D_80112340` ends the two-camera update loop. Existing `gRaceCamerasEnd` ends the four-camera array; preserve the distinct boundaries.
- Scheduler pool starts, render queue heads, and field aliases are views into larger objects. Preserve their relocation and overlapping-layout behavior.
- `D_800D6078` extends the prize table through cross-object indexing. Its physical adjacency matters.
- Unreferenced zero words do not establish padding. Reserved/trailing names remain provisional, and those objects should not be deleted on this evidence.
- Identical format strings have separate per-slot objects for matching layout. Keep separate copies.
- JP dispatch tables can reuse the existing US handler-table names after checking region-specific declarations. The proposals do not imply adding duplicate globals.
